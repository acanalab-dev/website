How much does it cost to make an affirmation app?

The answer ranges from nothing upfront to tens of thousands of dollars, depending on how you build it. Here's every cost, the ones people forget, and how acanalab's two plans compare.

Updated 2026-11-28

Short answer

Every iPhone app pays Apple $99 a year for the Developer Program, and Apple keeps 15% of subscription sales for most small developers. Building the app is the big variable: a custom app from an agency or freelancer usually costs tens of thousands of dollars plus paid updates, a no-code builder costs a monthly subscription and your own time, and a white-label service can cost nothing upfront. With acanalab, the Partner plan is $0 upfront and acanalab takes 30% of net sales; Pay once is a one-time fee ($4,900 regular price) and you keep 100% of sales.

Prices for custom work vary a lot by country, scope and who you hire, so get several quotes. A subscription app with widgets, reminders and a paywall is more work than it looks, and testing on different iPhones and iOS versions adds to it.

Costs people forget

  • iOS updates. Apple releases a new iOS every year. Apps sometimes need changes to keep working well and to keep passing review.
  • App Store assets. Screenshots, an icon in every size, a description and keywords.
  • Legal pages. A privacy policy and terms of use, hosted on a website.
  • Content. Hundreds of affirmations across many topics, written or licensed.
  • Rejections. If Apple asks for changes, someone has to make them and resubmit.
  • Your time. On a builder, you're doing all of the above yourself.

Worked example: Partner or Pay once?

Say your app has 200 subscribers at $7.99 a month. Apple pays out about $1,358 a month in net sales.

  • Partner: you keep about $951 a month and pay nothing upfront.
  • Pay once: you keep about $1,358 a month, or $407 more. At the $4,900 regular price, that difference pays back the fee in about 12 months.

With 500 subscribers, the difference grows to about $1,019 a month, and Pay once pays for itself in about 5 months. With 100 subscribers, it takes about two years. So the bigger and more engaged your audience, the more Pay once makes sense. If you're not sure how many people will subscribe, Partner keeps your risk at Apple's $99 a year.

How to choose

  • Testing an idea, or a smaller audience? Start with no upfront cost: acanalab Partner.
  • Large, engaged audience and confident it will sell? Pay once keeps the most money in your pocket.
  • Need features no existing app has? Get quotes for a custom build, and budget for upkeep.
  • Love building things yourself? A no-code builder can work, if it supports the features you need.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free way to make an affirmation app?

Not entirely: every iPhone app needs Apple's Developer Program at $99 a year. With acanalab's Partner plan there's no setup fee on top, and acanalab earns only a share of sales.

How much does acanalab cost?

Partner is $0 upfront, and you keep 70% of net sales. Pay once is a one-time fee, $4,900 regular price, and you keep 100% of sales. Either way you pay Apple $99 a year.

How much does Apple take from app subscriptions?

15% for developers in Apple's Small Business Program, which covers most developers earning under $1 million a year from the App Store, and 30% otherwise.

Do I pay acanalab every month?

On Pay once, no. On Partner, Apple pays you every month and you send acanalab 30% of net sales within 15 days, so you only pay when the app earns.

What does it cost to keep an app running?

Apple's $99 a year, plus updates for new iOS versions and fixes. With acanalab Partner, updates are free while acanalab's share averages $250 a month or more; with Pay once, fixes are free for 12 months.
