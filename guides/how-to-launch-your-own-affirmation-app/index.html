What every affirmation app needs

However you build it, an affirmation app on the App Store needs the same few things. It helps to know the list before you choose a route, because the route decides who does each part.

  • An Apple Developer account. $99 a year, paid to Apple. The app is published under this account, so whoever owns it owns the listing, the reviews and the subscribers.
  • A name, icon and colors. Your brand, so followers recognize the app as yours.
  • Affirmations. Hundreds of them, sorted into topics like confidence, calm, self-love and sleep, so people get something fresh every day.
  • Features that bring people back. A daily reminder, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, streaks, favorites and shareable cards. These are what keep subscribers paying month after month.
  • Subscriptions and a paywall. Sold through Apple's in-app purchases, which Apple requires for digital content in iPhone apps.
  • Legal pages and a listing. A privacy policy, terms of use, App Store screenshots, a description and Apple's privacy labels.
  • Apple's review. Every app and every update is checked by Apple before it goes live.

Step by step: from idea to the App Store

  1. Check that your audience fits. Affirmation apps suit mindset, wellness, self-care, faith, fitness and lifestyle audiences: people who already come to you for encouragement. Ask your followers whether they'd use a daily app from you. Our self-check on whether influencers should have their own app helps here.
  2. Pick your route. Custom, builder or white-label, based on your budget and how much work you want to do yourself.
  3. Choose a name, icon and colors. Keep the name short and close to your own brand, so people find it when they search the App Store for you.
  4. Set up your Apple Developer account. You can enroll as an individual or as a company. A company account shows your company name on the App Store and needs a free D-U-N-S number. Add your bank and tax details so Apple can pay you, and join Apple's Small Business Program to pay a 15% fee instead of 30%.
  5. Prepare the affirmations. Write your own, use a ready library, or mix both. Your own voice in a few categories makes the app feel personal.
  6. Set up subscriptions. Most affirmation apps are free to download, with an optional monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks widgets, more categories and more affirmations a day.
  7. Create the listing. Screenshots, a description, keywords, a privacy policy, terms of use and Apple's privacy labels.
  8. Submit to Apple. Apple reviews the app, often within a day or two. If it asks for changes, fix them and resubmit.
  9. Launch to your audience. Announce it, show it in stories and posts, and keep mentioning it. Most downloads come from you sharing it, not from App Store search.
  10. Keep it up to date. Each new iOS version can need updates, and fresh content keeps subscribers around.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Publishing under someone else's account. If the app isn't on your own Apple Developer account, the listing and the subscribers aren't really yours. Ask before you sign up.
  • Launching once and going quiet. An app grows when you keep mentioning it, just like any product.
  • Spending a big budget before testing demand. Start with a low-risk route, see how your audience responds, then invest more.
  • Forgetting the daily habit. Reminders, widgets and streaks matter more than extra features, because they're why people open the app every day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make an affirmation app without coding?

Yes. A no-code builder lets you assemble one yourself, and a white-label service like acanalab builds and publishes a finished app for you, so you only pick your branding and set up your Apple account.

Do I need a company to publish an app?

No. You can enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an individual. A company account shows your company name on the App Store and needs a free D-U-N-S number.

How long does it take to launch an affirmation app?

A custom build usually takes months. With a finished white-label app, most of the waiting is on setting up your Apple account and on Apple's review, which often takes a day or two.

Should my affirmation app be free or paid?

Most affirmation apps are free to download with an optional monthly or yearly subscription. A free download makes it easy for followers to try, and the subscription is how the app earns.
