How it works

Four steps, and no spreadsheets to keep up with.

  1. Add an expense

    Enter the amount, pick a type like groceries or rent, and choose who paid. Takes a few seconds. See how to track shared expenses.

  2. Invite your partner

    They scan a QR code from your iPhone, and you both see and add the same shared expenses.

  3. See who spent what

    HoneyNest totals what each of you paid and shows the split, like “Ana spent €140 more than Luka”.

  4. Even it out your way

    Use the difference to settle up however you like, or let it guide who pays next. See how to split expenses as a couple.

Questions

The short answers.

Is HoneyNest free?

Yes. HoneyNest is free to download, and adding expenses, partner sync, the spending split, recurring expenses and CSV export are all free. Premium unlocks your complete expense history.

How does my partner see the same expenses?

Go to Settings and invite your partner. They scan the QR code on your iPhone with HoneyNest, and from then on you both add and see the same shared expenses, synced through iCloud.

Where is my data stored?

In your own iCloud account. HoneyNest has no sign-up and no servers of its own that store your expenses, and the optional AI insights are generated on your iPhone. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do we both need an iPhone?

Yes. HoneyNest is an iPhone app that syncs through iCloud, so both partners need an iPhone signed in to iCloud to share expenses.

Which currencies does HoneyNest support?

You pick the currency symbol you use, such as $, €, £, ¥, CHF or kr, from more than 20 options. The symbol is for display only, and HoneyNest doesn't convert between currencies.
