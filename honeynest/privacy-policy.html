Privacy Policy

HoneyNest app · Last updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the HoneyNest app for iPhone (“the app”) handles your information. In short: your expenses live on your devices and in your own iCloud account, we don't run servers that store them, and there's no tracking and no third-party ads.

No account, no tracking

The app doesn't ask you to create an account or give us your email. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it doesn't use your data to follow you across other apps or websites.

What you enter

To work, the app stores what you add to it:

  • the names and colors you choose for you and your partner
  • your expenses: amount, title, type, date, who paid and whether it repeats
  • your settings, such as your currency symbol and chosen time period

This data is saved on your iPhone and in your private iCloud storage. We never receive it.

iCloud sync and sharing with your partner

The app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your data in sync and backed up under your Apple Account. When you invite your partner, the app uses iCloud sharing so that the person who accepts your invite can see and edit your shared data. Expenses in your Personal tab aren't part of the shared totals. You can stop sharing or remove your data in the app's Settings at any time. This data is stored by Apple and is not accessible to us. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Camera

If you join a partner, the app asks to use the camera to scan their invite QR code. The camera image is only used on your device to read the code, and it isn't saved or sent anywhere.

AI insights

On iPhones that support Apple's on-device AI, the app can write a short summary of your spending. It's generated entirely on your iPhone. Your data isn't sent to us or to any external AI service. AI can make mistakes, so please check important details yourself.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store, so Apple handles all payments. We never see your name, email or payment details.

To check which purchases you've made and unlock Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic technical information such as your device type, app version and country. It isn't linked to your identity, it doesn't receive your expenses, and it isn't used for tracking or advertising. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating prompt. Your rating and review go to Apple, not to us.

This website

The app itself has no analytics. These web pages (the app page, guides and this policy) use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies, doesn't fingerprint your device, and gives us only anonymous totals such as page views per page, referring site, country and device type. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings and use the delete option to permanently remove all people, expenses and settings, including your iCloud data. If you share with a partner, they lose access to the shared data too. Deleting the app alone removes its data from your iPhone but not from iCloud. You can also remove it in iOS Settings: tap your name, then iCloud and Manage Storage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Since we don't hold your expense data, there's usually nothing to request, but you're always welcome to ask.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at support@acanalab.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
