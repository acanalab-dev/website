Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers this website and our brand picker (the page where you design your app). It explains what happens to your information when you use them. We've kept it short, because we collect very little.

What we collect

Almost nothing. This website and the brand picker have no sign-up, no accounts, no cookies and no ads. We don't track you across sites and we don't build a profile of you.

Visit statistics

To see how many people visit our pages, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies or local storage and doesn't fingerprint your device. It counts page views together with basic, non-identifying details such as the page, the referring site, your country and your browser and device type. We only see these totals, never who you are. You can read how Cloudflare handles this in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, to keep the service secure and running. We don't receive or use this data. You can read how GitHub handles it in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The brand picker

The brand picker runs entirely in your browser. What you enter there, such as your app name, colors, texts and images, is saved only in your browser so it's still there when you come back. Nothing is sent to us until you download your picks file and choose to send it to us yourself. You can clear everything at any time with "Start over" or by clearing your browser's site data.

To show the handwritten font on the generated icon, the brand picker loads it from Google Fonts. This means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. See the Google Privacy Policy.

If you send us your picks, we use them only to build your app and talk to you about it.

Links to other sites

This website links to other sites, such as the App Store. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

If you contact us

If you write to us, we use your message and contact details only to reply and to discuss working together. We don't share them with anyone else or use them for marketing.

Apps we build for creators

This policy doesn't cover the apps we build. Each app is published by its creator and comes with its own privacy policy, which you can find on its App Store page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since this website collects none, there's usually nothing to request, but you're always welcome to ask.

Contact

Questions about this page? Write to us at support@acanalab.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
