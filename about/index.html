What is acanalab?

A small European studio that builds calm, privacy-friendly iPhone apps, and gives creators their own branded affirmations app.

Updated 2026-11-28

Short answer

acanalab is a small European app studio. It builds a finished daily affirmations app for iPhone with a creator's name, icon and colors, and publishes it under the creator's own Apple Developer account, so the app, the listing and the subscribers belong to the creator. There's no technical work for the creator. On the Partner plan there's no setup fee and the creator keeps 70% of net subscription sales; on Pay once the creator pays a one-time fee and keeps 100%. acanalab also publishes its own iPhone apps, Daily Affirmations and HoneyNest.

What acanalab does

acanalab gives creators and influencers their own daily affirmations app for iPhone. We build it with your name, icon and colors, publish it on the App Store under your own Apple Developer account, and keep it updated. Your followers get a daily dose of encouragement from you, right on their phones, and you earn from subscriptions every month.

  • What's in the app: hundreds of affirmations across many topics, a fresh one every day, streaks and gentle reminders, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, favorites, shareable cards, calm card styles and a short private reflection on favorites written on the iPhone.
  • Ownership: your Apple account, your listing, your reviews and your subscribers. Apple pays you directly.
  • Your work: pick your look, set up your Apple Developer account and share the app. No coding.
  • Privacy: no third-party ads and no tracking. We prepare a privacy policy and terms of use in your name.

See it working in Daily Affirmations, our own app on the App Store, which your app is built from.
