Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. The one thing you set up yourself is your Apple Developer account, in your name ($99 a year, paid to Apple). Because the app and its earnings are yours, Apple asks for your bank and tax details there so it can pay you directly, and you join its Small Business Program for the lower fee. It's filling in forms, not technical work. After that, you choose your look and approve the App Store listing, and we handle everything else. We're here whenever you need help, before launch and after.

Why does the app go on my own Apple developer account?

Because then it's truly yours. The app appears on the App Store under your name and your brand, and the listing, the reviews and the subscribers are all yours. Apple pays the money straight to you every month. Then, within 15 days, you send us 30% of net sales, or 50% until your app has made its first $10,000 in net sales.

What if my app doesn't earn much?

That’s okay. Sometimes an app just isn’t the right fit for a particular audience. Updates stay free while our share averages $250 or more a month, and below that we quote each one, only if you want it. If we both decide the app isn’t worth keeping, we simply remove it, with no penalty for either of us.

Do I have to write all the affirmations myself?

No. The app comes with hundreds of affirmations across many topics. You can add your own on top, and they appear in their own category with your name. But this is completely optional.

How long until my app is live?

We both want your app live as soon as possible, so we move quickly. It mostly depends on how soon we get your picks, content and Apple account access, and on Apple's review, which we don't control.

What do I need to do at launch?

Just do what you already do best: share it with your audience, your way. Like any launch, the first few weeks count the most. After that, it works best when it shows up naturally in your content.

Why not hire an agency or a developer?

Building a custom app with an agency or freelancer means a big upfront investment, a long wait, and paying again every time something needs updating. Here you get a finished, tested app with nothing to pay upfront, it's live much sooner, and updates are free while your app does well. We only earn when you earn, so we want your app to succeed as much as you do.

Can you guarantee downloads or income?

No one honestly can. How the app does depends on your audience and how you share it.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. We start with iPhone. Once your app does well there and we both agree Android makes sense, we’ll build it for you. There’s no setup fee for it either, just the same revenue share.

Are there other apps available?

Not yet, but we’re working on more apps for creators. Your Apple developer account will already be set up, so adding another app under your name is quick and easy, with no extra Apple fee.

What if my audience needs a different kind of app?

Tell us about it. If you have an idea your audience would love, write to us and we’ll talk it through together.

What about my followers' privacy?

The app shows no third-party ads and doesn't track people. We prepare a privacy policy and terms of use in your name and host them for you.
