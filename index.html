Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

What do I need to do?

Very little, and nothing technical. You set up an Apple Developer account in your name ($99 a year, paid to Apple) and add your bank and tax details, so Apple can pay you directly, and join its Small Business Program for the lower fee. It’s filling in forms, not technical work. Then you pick your look, approve the App Store listing and share the app with your audience, your way. The app comes with hundreds of affirmations, so adding your own is optional. We handle everything else, and we’re here whenever you need help.

Why does the app go on my own Apple developer account?

Because then it’s truly yours. The app appears on the App Store under your name and your brand, and the listing, the reviews and the subscribers are all yours. Apple pays the money straight to you every month. On Partner, you then send us 30% of net sales within 15 days.

Partner or Pay once: which should I pick?

Pick Partner if you’d rather not pay anything upfront: we share what the app earns and keep improving it for you. Pick Pay once if you’d rather keep every dollar. It pays off fastest with a large, engaged audience: with 500 subscribers, Pay once puts about $1,000 more in your pocket each month. With Pay once, fixes are included for 12 months, and new features are quoted separately, only if you want them.

What if my app doesn’t earn much?

No one can honestly guarantee downloads or income. It depends on your audience and how you share it. On Partner, your only cost is Apple’s $99 a year, and your audience still gets a daily dose of positivity from you. Updates stay free while our share averages $250 or more a month, and below that we quote each one, only if you want it. If we both decide the app isn’t worth keeping, we simply remove it, with no penalty for either of us.

How long until my app is live?

We both want your app live as soon as possible, so we move quickly. It mostly depends on how soon we get your picks, content and Apple account access, and on Apple’s review, which we don’t control. Like any launch, the first few weeks of sharing it count the most.

Why not hire an agency or a developer?

A custom app like this typically costs $40,000 or more to build with an agency or freelancer, takes months, and costs again every time something needs updating. Here you get a finished, tested app that’s live much sooner, either with no setup fee on Partner or for a one-time $4,900 with Pay once.

Is there Android, or other kinds of apps?

Not yet. We start with iPhone, and once your app does well there and we both agree Android makes sense, we’ll build it for you. We’re also working on more apps for creators, and since your Apple developer account is already set up, adding another under your name is quick, with no extra Apple fee. If your audience would love a different kind of app, write to us and we’ll talk it through.

What about my followers’ privacy?

The app shows no third-party ads and doesn’t track people. We prepare a privacy policy and terms of use in your name and host them for you.
