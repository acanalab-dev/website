Brand your app

Design your own affirmations app in a few minutes.

1App

Try to stay below 15 characters, ideally below 12, so the whole name fits under the icon.

Do not use special characters or emojis. Please note that Apple can reject a name for various reasons, for example if it is too close to or the same as an existing app.

Emoji can't be used in the app name, so we removed it.
On some iPhones this name may not fit under the icon and will end in "…". A shorter name always shows in full.

Added after your app name in the App Store, like "Daily Affirmations - Your Best". The full App Store name, including the dash between the two, can be up to 30 characters.

App Store name:

The short line under your name in the App Store, like "Positive Thinking & Self Care".

App icon

One to two short words work best. Each word gets its own line, like "From Jane".
